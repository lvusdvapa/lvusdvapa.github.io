Our Stages

01 Who’s renting?

Your tier sets every rate below. Most outside groups fall into Tier III or IV.

02 Spaces

Enter rental hours — your full time in the space. For the MainStage and Black Box, also enter show hours, the time an audience is present, which sets front-of-house staffing.

03 Staffing

Front of house matches your show hours; back of house and custodial match total space hours. Set how many staff you need. Admin & box office is a flat fee on every booking.

04 Equipment

Daily or per-use fees. Set the number of days where it applies.

Included Comes with the space, no charge

Stage & lighting

  • Black and white scrims (MainStage)
  • Cyclorama (Black Box)
  • Followspots
  • Intelligent lighting fixtures
  • Mirror ball
  • Antari F3 hazer, Rosco 1800 fogger
  • Pipe and drape, as available

Tables & chairs

  • 8-ft and 6-ft tables

Audio

  • 16 wireless handheld mics
  • 16 wireless lavalier mics
  • 16 DPA headset mics
  • Yamaha M7CL-48 ES console
  • Drum kit microphones

Additional backline and instruments available on request.

Filming: the base rate covers a six-hour minimum; time past six is billed at the additional-hour rate.
Pianos: rates don’t include tuning, which is billed separately.
Annual escalation: rates rise each year by the greater of 3% or CPI.
